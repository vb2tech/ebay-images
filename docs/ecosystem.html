How a lot moves

photos → eBay
IN

Photos in chat

Drop slab photos, front and back, in any chat in this project.

1

Upload lot photos

One folder per slab in the repo with a listing.json.

status → new
2

Pricing proposal

Sold comps, a numbered review page, your approval in chat.

new → priced
3

Create bulk upload

Fills eBay's template with every priced item and runs the validator.

priced → exported
OUT

Seller Hub

You upload the .xlsx, then say it went up or share eBay's results file.

exported → uploaded

The bulk upload validator runs inside step 3 and on its own for any file. Rows eBay rejects go back to priced and are retried in the next batch.

Bulk upload validator

Ready
bulk-upload-validator
Does
Checks any filled .xlsx or .csv against eBay's rules and the project standard. Verdict: PASS, PASS WITH WARNINGS or FAIL, with a fix per problem.
Produces
A report with errors, warnings and field coverage, plus fields ready to promote to the standard.
